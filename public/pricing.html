Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Yes! Pro and Premium plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex) and PayPal through our secure Stripe integration.

Is my financial data secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption (256-bit SSL) and never sell your data. We're GDPR compliant and SOC 2 certified.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, we'll refund your payment, no questions asked.

Do you offer student or non-profit discounts?

Yes! We offer 50% off Pro and Premium plans for students and non-profits. Contact us for verification.
